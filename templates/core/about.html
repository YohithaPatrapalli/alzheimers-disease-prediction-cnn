About the Prediction System

A research and educational project for exploring MRI image classification with convolutional neural networks.

What it does

The system lets users upload MRI images, run them through an available trained model, review prediction confidence scores, and revisit previous results and analytics. Results depend on the selected model and the quality of the input image.

Image predictions

Submit an MRI image and review the model's predicted category and confidence distribution.

Prediction history

Return to saved results and view aggregate prediction activity in the analytics dashboard.

Model management

Manage available models and datasets used by the application's prediction workflow.

Important: Predictions are for research and educational use only. They are not a medical diagnosis and should not be used to guide clinical decisions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.
